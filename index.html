Control, naturally

A gesture becomes an action.

AirControll turns your Mac camera into a local gesture interface powered by Apple Vision—without sending or saving camera frames.

01

Teach your gesture.

Calibrate built-in gestures or record unlimited custom gestures using multiple stable samples.

02

Choose an action.

Map gestures to media, volume, brightness, Mission Control, apps or complex keyboard shortcuts.

03

Move and control.

Use continuous recognition or trigger one action and automatically return the app to rest.

Built for real use

Control your Mac without touching it.

Use either hand, restrict recognition to left or right, and choose how long the camera stays active.

✓Left, right or both-hand recognition
✓Continuous and one-time measurement modes
✓Built-in and unlimited custom gestures
✓Temporal stability and duplicate-trigger prevention
Built to stay local

Privacy is the architecture.

Camera frames exist only in RAM, are analyzed locally and are discarded immediately. AirControll stores only normalized numeric gesture features.

Zero network processingNo account, server, upload or cloud inference.
No camera archiveNo photos, video, JPEG, PNG or HEIF files are created.
Camera released when idleRecognition stops and the capture session is released when disabled.
